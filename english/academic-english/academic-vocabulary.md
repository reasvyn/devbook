### How to Use This Guide

This document is not meant to be read from beginning to end in a single sitting. Use it as a reference: when you encounter an unfamiliar word in a DevBook subject, look it up here. Each vocabulary table includes the word, its part of speech, a concise definition, and an example sentence drawn from the relevant academic domain.

Words marked with an asterisk (\*) are part of the Academic Word List (AWL) — the 570 most frequent words in academic English across all disciplines. Mastering these words alone will dramatically improve your reading comprehension.

Academic English uses transitional phrases to make the logical structure of arguments explicit. These phrases signal relationships between claims, evidence, and conclusions.

## Learning Tips

Mastering academic vocabulary is a process of exposure, not memorization. The following strategies will accelerate your acquisition.

**Read actively in the target discipline.** The most effective way to learn academic vocabulary is to encounter it in context. When reading a philosophy or psychology document in DevBook, do not skip unfamiliar words. Pause, look them up in this guide, and read the example sentence.

**Use spaced repetition.** Create a digital flashcard deck (Anki, RemNote, or similar) with the words from this guide. Review new cards daily and older cards at increasing intervals. Focus on words from subjects you are actively studying.

**Learn word families, not isolated words.** When you encounter an unfamiliar word, learn its entire family. If you see "hypothesis," learn "hypothesize," "hypothetical," and "hypothetically" at the same time. This multiplies your vocabulary growth per unit of effort.

**Study transitional phrases separately.** Transitional phrases are the scaffolding of academic arguments. Practice writing paragraphs that use different categories: addition, contrast, cause-effect, and summary. The ability to produce these phrases actively improves your reading comprehension passively.

**Focus on the Academic Word List first.** The 570 words of the AWL represent the most frequent academic words across all disciplines. Every general academic word in this guide (marked with \*) belongs to the AWL. Master these before moving to subject-specific vocabulary.

**Use the glossary as a quick reference.** When reading a DevBook subject, keep this document open. When you encounter an unfamiliar word, search this guide. The table format allows rapid lookup.
